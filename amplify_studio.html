⬡ Ember Forge
Voice-First AI Companion RPG
The forge reads you. It asks questions with no right answer. From your answers it shapes a companion who never existed before. Together you sit by a fire that wasn't burning until you arrived, and you discover what kind of story you're inside.

Build a party. Lead them through dice-driven encounters narrated by The Keeper. Forge mementos from shared moments. Watch the world get harder. Lose people. Find new ones. The game remembers everything. This is not a game you win. This is a game that remembers.
