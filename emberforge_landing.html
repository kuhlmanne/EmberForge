EMBER FORGE
every voice carries weight
The forge reads you. It asks questions with no right answer. From your answers it shapes a companion who never existed before. Together you sit by a fire that wasn't burning until you arrived.

Build a party. Lead them through dice-driven encounters. Forge mementos from shared moments. Watch the world get harder. Lose people. Find new ones.

This is not a game you win. This is a game that remembers.
